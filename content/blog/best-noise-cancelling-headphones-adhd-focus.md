## Why ANC Helps ADHD {#why}

ADHD brains struggle to filter auditory distractions. Background noise causes cognitive overload. ANC microphones detect ambient sound and generate inverse sound waves, canceling noise 25–35 dB—roughly 80% noise reduction.

**Result:** ADHD users focus 40–60% longer with ANC on.

Research shows 70% of ADHD users report improved focus with noise-cancelling headphones. Adding lo-fi or instrumental music amplifies this benefit.

## What to Look For {#what}

✅ **ANC quality:** 25–35 dB reduction  
✅ **Comfort:** Wearable for 4+ hours without fatigue  
✅ **Sound:** Neutral or warm (avoid harsh highs)  
✅ **Ambient mode:** Hear alarms without removing headphones  
✅ **Battery:** 20+ hours (lasts workweek)  
✅ **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.3+, stable

- Nature sounds (rain, forest)
- Brown noise (low-frequency white noise)

- Podcasts (too engaging)
- Anything with lyrics (distracting)

## FAQ {#faq}

### Can I use ANC for 8+ hours daily?

Yes, but take 10-minute breaks each hour. Continuous ANC can feel fatiguing.

### Do I need music with ANC?

No. Many ADHD users prefer silence + ANC only. Music is optional.

### Best for glasses wearers?

Bose and Sony have minimal temple pressure—ideal for glasses.
